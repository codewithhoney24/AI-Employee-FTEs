# K-Electric Pricing Structure

## Residential (Domestic)
- Rate: 25 PKR per unit (kWh)
- Base charge: 500 PKR/month
- Min usage: 100 units/month

## Commercial
- Rate: 20 PKR per unit
- Base charge: 2,000 PKR/month
- Min usage: 500 units/month

## Industrial
- Rate: 18 PKR per unit
- Base charge: 5,000 PKR/month
- Min usage: 5,000 units/month

## Late Payment
- After 5 days: +5% penalty
- After 15 days: +10% penalty + suspension warning
- After 30 days: Service suspension

## Discounts
- Payment within 3 days: 2% discount
- Referral bonus: 500 PKR per new customer
- Volume bulk (>10,000 units): Special negotiation
